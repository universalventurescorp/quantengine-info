Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

QuantEngine uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to, and read the list of videos of, the YouTube channel owned by its developer (@QuantEngineOfficial).

QuantEngine does not collect, store, sell or share personal data from any other user. The OAuth access token is stored privately by the developer and is used solely to publish videos to the developer's own channel.

QuantEngine's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
